# Cat Food owner-plan fixture

`catfood-plan.tsv` is the byte form emitted by Cat Food
`android/plan-build.sh` at
`b387f90d3973aae39ba19d370a830da627299ab1` for the Wegert application
contract retained beside it and source
`873d03e86ec2e4a9236bb2d253fee3c8fde83b6c`. The source identity is real;
the fixture remains a host-side contract record and makes no build, package,
installation, launch, or physical-device claim.

The owner-state test supplies independently authored current-cell and attempt
projections around these exact plan bytes. Those projections exercise
Cockswain's consumer. They do not add cells to Cat Food or certify a runtime.
